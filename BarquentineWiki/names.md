- **type:** `pc`, `npc`, `place`, `item` or `faction`.
- **first seen:** the earliest episode that names it, found by grepping every S2 transcript with the variants below. A name with no S2 mention keeps its S3 episode.
- **transcript variants:** how the captions mangle it, for grepping the raw VTT.
