# Threads

The storyline ledger. Each thread lists every episode that touches it, so a callback can be traced back to its setup. Recap frontmatter cites these ids in `threads_opened`, `threads_advanced` and `threads_closed`, and `tools/lint_recaps.R` checks that the thread lists that episode.

- **Format:** a `## T-### · title` heading, then a `status:` line (`open` or `closed`), then one `- sXXeYY — what happened` bullet per episode.
- **Spoiler rule:** prose in a recap is written as of its own episode. Payoffs live here.
- These are S3 seeds. E1 adds the S2 setups.
