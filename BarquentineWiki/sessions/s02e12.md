# I'm Not Sure What I've Gotten Us Into

*S2E12 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E12 Im not sure what ive gotten us into.transcript.vtt`*

The ship and crew erupt through the belly of a whale onto the waters surface!

With the ship raised, Basil assumes his promised captaincy. But at what cost? The Ki-Rin *is* the Manticore and its sights are set on chaos and destruction.

They encounter a documentarian out to sea along with his very-similar-looking crew. In exchange for their story, Davitt the documentarian supplies them with some food and broadcasts what they shared of their tales back to somewhere that it was being shared with other people.

With Davitt departed, they all tried some sort of way to get close to the ship.

Room cast mending on a broken plank, and the ship rejected it. Lumi cast light on the helm; the ship fought him, and when he forced it, the light alerted something in the distance and Basil came down to scold him and snuff it. Cyan didn't try anything at all. He sent for help: this thing is cursed.

And it's with that last blink of light, and Basil's insistence to snuff it, that we heard a noise in the distance, a call…
