# I'm Not Your Buddy, Pal

*S2E4 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E4.transcript.vtt`*

The team fought their way through the tower guards, finding their gear along the way. Picking up their gear, Phthalos equipping himself with a massive anchor and a belt that seems to enlarge his muscles _even more_.

Room lifts a few magical items on their way out but the hard-fought battle is yet to conclude. Basil's mind is dominated, Room casts confusion, and the map room is scattered into a brawl that leaves a few dead, a few surviving without clothes, and our adventuring team headed off to the roof where they take in the true scope of what's left to be done:

A torrential storm surrounds the giant tower prison floating on the ocean, unleashing a relentless downpour of rain that pounds the stone walls with ferocious intensity. Gale-force winds whip through the air, howling like a chorus of vengeful spirits, while thunder booms ominously and lightning cracks the darkened sky, illuminating the storm's wrath in fleeting, blinding flashes. The storm churns the ocean into a tempest, waves crashing violently against the tower's base, making the descent treacherous and escape to the ships below nearly impossible.

Escape the Monolith, escape the storm, escape the chase.
