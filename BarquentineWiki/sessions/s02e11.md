# My A Is Getting E'd

*S2E11 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E11 My A is Getting E'd.transcript.vtt`*

Somewhere under a full moon, behind a steel door with a red light over it, the crew take four seats on *The Pack Talks*, the werewolf talk show hosted by Ciaran Silverhowl. It's a flash-forward. The globoid friend once called Blue introduces himself as Cyan, and he is now that color, wearing a belt. Basil is captain of a party that has a ship now. Lumi is a fallen child of the heavens, Room is a loxodon in an owl costume, and Ted cheers from the wings.

The interview turns into group therapy. Lumi calls Room out for shoving one skeleton into shallow water and leaving him to the arrows, and Room thanks him for getting them out alive. Cyan tells how he beat his brother into submission and then killed him, and how he feels now: *"greener than I used to."* In the lightning round every right answer is "wolf," and only Cyan gets them all. Room wild shapes into a wolf for one howl, then tears off his easy ripoff jeans for a sponsor. When asked what comes next, Basil wants to help his family, and Lumi wants to know why his father's torso was in a chest.

Then comes the show's most important segment, *"how tasty are our panel today."* Silverhowl turns into a werewolf, more werewolves leap on stage, and the audience wears shirts that say "eat them." Lumi's cloudkill would gas his own friends, so he passes on it. Basil's glittering aquamarine hand crushes two werewolves, Room holds two more in place and brains the host with a chair, and Cyan punches. With his last spell slot, Basil drives a burst of wooden spikes up through the stage and into Silverhowl's heart.

The last werewolf flees into the dark. The house lights come up, the audience is gone, and all that's left is a chest. What's inside? How did the crew get from a flooding cave to this stage? And who booked this interview? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
