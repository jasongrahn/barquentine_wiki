# This Guy You Know

*S2E9 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E9 This guy you know.transcript.vtt`*

The crew come across Plug's butt ugly stuff ship, a wholly owned subsidiary of Plugs butt ugly stuff hut. The group purchasing helmet, air pump, and tubing to craft a makeship diving suit. On Lumi asking Plug for books about Vecna, the codger warned to keep that name quiet and started to close shop, but not before a few more purchases were made.

They tried to vote their way around the prophecy Basil received from his patron to find - no dice.

But then… Basil touched the two compass needles to each other. The compasses grabbed hold of each other through miniature tentacles, disassembling, and reforming into a 3 dimensional gyrocompass. It's at that moment that the weather turns violent and a whirlpool sucks them to the seafloor and sweeps them into a cavern, unconscious.

The dimly lit cave leads them to a shallow boat and canal. As they travel, they're given unsettling visions of an underwater world filled with impossible geometries, twisted ships, and ghostly figures. At the end of the canal was a small dock, leading them to a much more massive cavern, within it an aged ship dimly lit by the bioluminescence of the cave.

Phthalos makes his way toward the ship and Blue follows in-step. The two race ahead, Basil close on their heels.

As the three board the ship, a semi-translucent bubble forms over the ship; locking Room, Lumi, and poor ol' Ted out on the boardwalk as skeletal figures size from shambled piles.
