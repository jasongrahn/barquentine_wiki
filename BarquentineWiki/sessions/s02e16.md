# Everyone's Gonna See Me Naked

*S2E16 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E16 Everyones gunna see me naked.transcript.vtt`*

The throne is ready. Large Luigi stages a grand unveiling at Bed Bath & Beholder, behind a red velvet curtain, and when Basil pulls the rope the whole shop gasps. It's the most grotesque chair on Bral, built from the bones of everything Luigi ever disintegrated. The seat and back are one great egg, with a hole in the back in the shape of an eye. It's a beholder's skull. Lumi remembers that every beholder thinks it's the pinnacle of its kind, so the chair is both a flex and a compliment, and any other beholder who sees it means trouble. Basil loves it. The ship, he tells Luigi, needs two things to get back to its former glory: this throne, and probably *"an unfortunate amount of"* blood. Ted floats it home on a Tenser's disc. Then he climbs on and sits in it before the captain does.

Recruiting night. Ted's hand-drawn flyers are all over town, some with Billow, some without, some drawn under Room's guidance and looking sick as hell. When Basil says the ship's name out loud in Luigi's shop, the room goes quiet and people start leaving. So the crew keep the name quiet and hold the open call at Tiny's Tipsy by the Sea, the first dive off the docks, where every chair is on a spring, the oyster shells go on the floor, and you never, ever order anything that didn't come out of the ocean.

Basil goes first. Robert, a trobster (part triton, part lobster), lifts his goblet with his mind and wants something darker than money. Basil offers him the astral sea: no water, just vacuum and withering cold, ports unknown, treasure without names. *Where do I sign?* Lumi takes on a trio of eager amateurs, Lock, Stock and Barrel, whose only brush with true terror was a mother-in-law. He tells them flatly that they're in no position to negotiate, and they sign. Room closes on Cletus, still in his Furrizon Wireless pullover, who has brought Sirvellica along: a goth in black with a white staff, who owes Cletus 20 gold, can sew a slip cover for anything and insults everyone she meets. Will the two of them be a problem on a long voyage? She has an answer for that: *"You guys play your cards right, everybody's going to see me naked."*

Six new hands, for 15 gold and 30 gold and the price of Cletus's heart. The throne is aboard and the crew is whole: twelve. What does a ship like the Manticore make of a throne made of bones? Will Robert get the darkness he came for? And who exactly has the crew just hired? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
