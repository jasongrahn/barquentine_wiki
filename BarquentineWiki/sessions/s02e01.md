# Definitely Not Full of Muscles

*S2E1 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E1 Definitely not full of muscles.transcript.vtt`*

The crew finds themselves stripped of their belongings aboard a transport called The Sentinel, destined for the prison known only as The Monolith. The prison seems to be their next stop in finding Basil's patron ship, chasing after a dangerous green plasmoid known as Phthalos. the Pyxis no longer among their ranks, they find a new compatriot in the fallen Aasimar known as Luminariel.

Through meditation, Blue has a vision of another, an armor-clad red plasmoid called Hierophant Vermilion. This is the second time he's seen Vermilion in a vision, both lay as secrets he's kept to himself.

The Monolith is a single solitary tower, suspended above the ocean by 16 ships each providing an opposed pulling force against another through chain and ironworks. Each ship with their own regiment of sailors and arcanists working around the clock to keep the prisoners from a watery grave.

Blue, Basil, Room, and Luminariel are power stunned and taken up the outside of the monolith on floating disks. Then through a series of doors, and lowered through an aperture to the prison floor approximately 200 feet below. It's here they meet Phthalos - a hulk and a brute compared to Blue's nimble and soft gelatinous form. He's also.. kind of an asshole.
