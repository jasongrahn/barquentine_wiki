# You Came Back

*S3E7 · recorded 2026-11-17 · source: `GMT20261118-021523_Recording.transcript.vtt`*

One dragon. Three exhausted heroes. And no worm coming, no matter how loud Room screamed for it. So they fought her the hard way, inside her own lair, while the lair fought back. It slowed them, poisoned them, and breathed a nightmare that sent Lumi's nerve skittering across the glass. Room took to the air as a great yellow eagle and came down in an explosion of feathers. Lumi's floating gyoza kept spitting hot oil. And the Captain summoned a fist of water, punched the dragon in the face, caught her by the tail, and slammed her into the glass.

Mira stopped. Her jaw hung open on a roar that never came. Frost crept out of her mouth, scale by scale, until the dragon was the finest piece in her own collection. Then she hit the floor and shattered. Nothing left bigger than a fist. All around the cavern, the glass went soft. Lock, Stock and Barrel. Ted, with exactly one word. Sirvellica, with opinions. One by one, the crew walked free. Last came Elder Abarat, one stiff step out of his pillar. He looked at them for a long moment. *"You came back."*

Beyond the lair lay a mountain of gold. Giff gold, melted and fused, dug out of the glass one plate at a time and hoarded in the dark. Everyone pulled something from it. Lumi pulled out a sword, and the black veins in his body ran down his arm to meet it. He didn't put it down. Victoria dug out her iron chest, with the key still inside.

At the heart of it all, sealed in glass, stood the first Warforged, palm open. Beside it was a man reaching for the heavens. He had Lumi's face. Abarat's hand stopped at the glass a finger's width from that palm, and he wept. He told the story he'd told a hundred times. Then he told the one he never had: two of them set out together, he let go, and only he came back. Lumi's rib cage hummed. Room's map lit up. And the glass let Lumi's hand through, into the stone palm. It fit. It had always fit. Abarat turned to the bronze in Lumi's chest. *"Unifier?"*

Who is the man with Lumi's face? Why does Lumi's hand fit where Abarat's never could? And who, or what, exactly, is a Unifier? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
