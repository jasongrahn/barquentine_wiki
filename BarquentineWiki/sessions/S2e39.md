## Summary

[[Basil|the Captain]] recounted his brig visit to the group: his parents were found in commoner's clothes, worn but defiant. They confessed to discovering a mysterious artifact on a scarred planet — a sphere controlled through will alone — and admitted: "We built a weapon. But this artifact is merely a key." [[Basil|the Captain]] attempted to reveal his face to them but rolled only an 11 on his Performance check; they did not recognize him.

Back aboard the [[War Saint]]'s ship, [[Lumi]] accidentally blurted out sensitive information about [[Basil|the Captain]]'s parents, causing the slipped seed to spread through the gathered petitioners and triggering a panic throughout the flotilla. [[Room]] transformed slowly and horrifically into a giant spider, clearing the ship of bystanders with screams.

[[Basil|the Captain]] learned the truth about banishment: prisoners are sent to a glassed wasteland planet where the [[Giff]] can "collect them" later if needed. Outside, [[Lumi]] activated his Necrotic Shroud against a forming mob before [[Basil|the Captain]] dimension-doored them both to safety. [[Room]] attempted to wild-shape into a giant eagle to escape, but flight does not function as expected in the [[Astral Plane]]; guards seized him mid-air. He was chained and placed in the brig — in a cell next to [[Basil|the Captain]]'s parents.

The pieces are now accidentally in position. A jailbreak and heist are imminent.

## GM Notes
A jailbreak mini-game has been introduced with explicit mechanics: players choose a direction at each node, roll a d100, and navigate the result. Two failure conditions track progress — number of times guards get the better of the party, and time elapsed. Both have hard limits. Two routes are available: the outer deck (faster, more exposed, harder) and the bilge (slower, quieter, more rolls, higher variance). Loot is seeded along the route. Running and fighting both carry costs. The [[Manticore]] is the endpoint.
