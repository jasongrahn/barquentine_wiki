# It's the Notes You Don't Play

*S2E19 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E19 It's the notes you DONT play.transcript.vtt`*

A year ago to the day, Room walked through a mysterious gate and woke up on a strange world. He still doesn't know the way home. Around a rotting table in the stateroom, the crew finally get jobs: Elder Abarat as chief engineer, Robert as bosun, Silent Jim at navigation, Ted in the galley, loud Sirvellica up in the crow's nest, and Billow as chief morale officer. Then it's off to the Elven Rodeo, where the band plays one single note and the crowd goes wild. Lumi and Room stop dead and hear it. It's all about the notes you don't play. Basil does not hear it.

Mr. Catastrophe has a plan, and a warning: *"it's one thing to enter a dream, it is something else entirely to bring something out."* Room can't enter his own dream. Basil and Lumi will go in alone, and they'll have to work out how to talk to a friend who isn't there. It's big magic. It needs a full moon and every one of the elf's linked selves. If one of them gets out, he can pull them both out. Room tells his friends they can back out, no hard feelings. They don't.

There's one problem left: werewolf bites, maybe, from a game show, hypothetically. A bushy-mustached cleric at the healing hut lifts the curse for 900 gold, with a blessing that sounds a lot like a bathtime rhyme. The door slams, and there's snickering behind it. Room's verdict: they were taken for a ride. When Lumi suggests killing the cleric to get the money back, the mousekin's two scraps in Lumi's pocket go warm. At moonrise, on the Rodeo's roof, under the eyes of fifty elves, the three of them count down from ten. 

Basil and Lumi wake on a fortress roof. The world is in gray tones, with jazz playing somewhere. Downstairs, Large Luigi pours beers at the Happy Beholder, and every elephant in the room is a different loxodon. A voice in their heads offers a job. Room can only speak through his spells, so he heats their mugs, then boils their beer. Two hooded, beaked figures slip out the back. Out in the alley wait Gene and Skid, dohwar, penguins with hands, who finish each other's sentences. They take the crew out along a dock that ends in wild space, aboard a ship shaped like a dohwar sliding on its belly, to meet President Mario of the Silver Beak cartel. Vampirates seized his compound. His family's moon-cake recipe sits in a vault behind his chief of security's traps, and *"the recipe is a fiercely guarded secret not even all of the bakers know the ingredients."* Is the recipe the map? Who is Dread Captain Yi? And did that remove curse even work? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
