# On a Very Special Episode of Blossom

*S2E14 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E14 On a Very Special episode of Blossom.transcript.vtt`*

Mr. Catastrophe keeps his word and gates the crew to Hoovale, a village buried in snow on a winter night. No lights, no songs, no children. The bartender says it in verse: the kids were stolen from their beds by a being most foul who loathes Christmas. Then the door slams open, and a four-foot red plasmoid in shining armor clanks up to the bar. Hierophant Vermilion. He asks Cyan where Phthalos is. Cyan tells him the truth: he beat their brother to death. Vermilion takes it in stride. The town says a lich lives in the hills.

Roosevelt, the one kid who escaped, points them up the mountain with the smiling face carved into it. On the trail, snowmen rise from the drifts, pelting them with rock-hard snowballs, until Cyan kicks the ice off one and finds a missing villager inside. Mid-fight, Lumi reaches into his pack and pulls out something he never put there: a robe of useful items, with a note from his mom. When Basil recharges with the Rod of the Pack Keeper, the ship carved on it looks worse for wear, now bolted with plating.

In the cave, kobolds in red Christmas hats whip chained children building hideous toys. Room charms the last of them, hands out presents, and walks them straight into Lumi's angelic guardians. Through the next door, by a tree hung with skulls and snakes, the "lich" in red and white blasts the whole crew with cold. Cyan's fists knock off its skeletal mask: a mortal face, no lich at all, a man trying to buy immortality. Lumi's giant gyoza swallows him whole.

The village throws a feast. Cyan pulls his brother aside, and Vermilion lays it out: he has found a path, through faith, to whoever made them. Come with me, or stay with the family you've found. *"I have to go. I have to know."* Cyan leaves a note, *"here my path diverges,"* and the two ride off into the snow. Only Basil catches the sound of hooves. What did Vermilion find? Will Cyan ever come back to the Manticore? And what's happened to the ship on Basil's rod? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
