# Baku, Come Eat My Dreams

*S3E1 · recorded 2026-08-12 · source: `GMT20260812-012252_Recording.transcript.vtt`*

Wild space. The Manticore is between stops, bound for the marble, and everyone aboard is somewhere else in their heads. Room, bent over his charts in the navigator's chamber, remembered being small on a world that was one enormous city. He'd had a bad dream: he jumped, and he never came back down. His grandmother told him the birds aren't afraid of going up; they're off on a grand adventure. Then she gave him what her own grandmother had given her, for nights when no one else is there: *"Baku, come eat my dreams."*

Lumi, buried in his tomes, remembered the one day his adopted father let him into the office. Names streamed down a scroll. You etch one, dispatch it, and in a crystal on the wall a shadow arrives to pull a soul from its body. Then the scroll goes on the spike. Job done. Lumi wanted that job more than anything. From his room he heard what came next: his parents worrying about how fascinated their wingless son was with Philip, the one who collects the souls.

The Captain stood with his ear pressed to the captain's-quarters door, the one that has never opened for him, and remembered his first rank. It was deckhand, with a bucket and a scrub brush that each had his name riveted on. To earn it, he had to name everyone above him and everyone below him, down to a four-hundred-pound rock golem and a four-year-old cheering at the back. His big sister Gloria knelt down and gave him his first pipe. Then she saluted him.

Then a string of floating barge shops drifted into view, and the crew went shopping. Theodore, the wondrous-items merchant, talked like a game-show host and haggled like a shark. Room traded an 8th-level spell scroll and a pile of gold for a portable hole and a hat of disguise. Lumi traded a cloak born of a dream for a coin of infernal iron with a soul still trapped inside. The Captain bought an eyepatch for the eye he lost on the Flotilla. Lock, Stock, and Barrel took the Captain's wink as permission. And somewhere deeper in the void, something burrows through a planet, leaving behind a tunnel, and bodies encased in its walls.

What did Lock, Stock, and Barrel bring back? Whose soul is rattling around in Lumi's pocket? And what's waiting in the tunnels of the next world? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
