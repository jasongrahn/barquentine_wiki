# Hair on Edge

*S2E22 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E22 Hair on Edge.transcript.vtt`*

Battered, low on spells, and face to face with a young lunar dragon, the crew decide to talk. The fey spirit prostrates itself. Lumi dims the lights and kneels. Basil asks the dragon's name: Calisterics. It is lying on a hoard of moon-cake crates and wonders aloud whether they'd make better food. Room's druidcraft fills the vault with the smell of fresh bread, then snickerdoodles. Lumi explains that the bakers just want their recipe back. The dragon decides the recipe is in its hoard, so it's the dragon's. From outside the dream, Room spots the scroll on a wheelbarrow. Basil reaches it, reads the heading (the recipe for sweet honeyfish moon cakes), then fumbles the grab with a natural 1 and starts admiring the wheelbarrow's mahogany. Calisterics rears up, ready to fight for the nest where its hatchlings will come.

Lumi casts spirit guardians for the first time. One angelic being rises from his mother's necklace, and then the symbol turns black and a second, darker thing arrives. Room trades his fey for the spirit of a sapphire dragon, who scolds Calisterics for keeping such a poor lair. Then Lumi bets the gyoza on the hoard as bait and casts banishment. The dragon fails the save by one and vanishes for a minute. A pleasant voice fills the room. It's Periwinkle, who had planned to wait the dragon out. *"Take as many moon cakes as you can carry, but leave the recipe behind and I'll make sure you're handsomely rewarded by the red flag fleet."* The recipe might be Room's map, so Lumi tucks it into his robe.

Out the tunnel and onto the asteroid, they see the Raft coming in to land three hundred feet away. Behind them, a galleon called the Heartless rises under a blood-gray flag. On its bow, Dread Captain Yi stands in a tricorn hat, and beside her Periwinkle, a pudgy dohwar, points a cutlass at them. Vampirates swarm the rigging. Basil and Lumi run on their cloaks of the bat and dodge harpoons, icy geysers, a thrown bottle and one mutinous vampirate fired from a mangonel. Room's sapphire dragon harries the deck, shouting at Periwinkle, until the ship shrinks into a vast ooze, dissolves the dragon and starts eating the dream. Everything behind it goes black.

Room knows this dream. He whispers for Baku, and something answers: feed me your dreams. *"Baku, come eat my dreams, but a little slower, please."* A gust of wind and an elephant's battle roar buy a few feet. Lumi refuses to look back. Basil, at the hatch, can't look away, and his single curl of hair stands straight up. The gangplank pulls in, the hatch closes, and the three of them wake. Basil and Lumi remember everything. Room remembers nothing, but he has been touched by the fey. Is the recipe the map home? What is the thing that eats Room's dreams? And will Periwinkle really be in touch? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
