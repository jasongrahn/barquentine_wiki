# To Be 16 Again

*S2E30 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E30 To Be 16 Again.transcript.vtt`*

Lock, Stock and Barrel return from a store full of generals with four barrels of ten and no cash register. Basil sends Room to fetch Sirvellica and Cletus from a furniture store. Lumi takes Ted Two to check on the passengers, and through the hold door the green one probes his mind. He promises them a feast once they shove off and posts Ted Two at the door. On his way back up, he forgets who he was going to find.

In his cabin, Lumi asks the ship for help. Manny wants the frogs dead and Lumi as captain. Would the words in his pocket work? Read them, says Manny. Lumi reads "vile darkness," and everything goes silent. A too-tall shadow waves, then reaches out and taps his forehead. When sound returns, a rain cloud hangs over his head. Lumi tells Basil about the cloud and the ship. Through the helm, Basil hears the frogs plotting in a language that isn't Abyssal, Common, Giff or Orc, the green one giving orders. They'll wait for Room, cast off, and deal with the frogs in space. Then Lumi admits the ship wants him as captain.

Barrel admits the trio are in arrears with the generals. They claimed to be the ship's captains, stole the barrels, and told everyone the ship eats people. A torch-lit mob of nearly sixty crowds the pier, and in it Lumi spots someone else under a cloud. Basil pours fog from the headless figurehead: *"Who dares challenge the Manticore and her captain?"* The bar's owner wants the heads of the captains who robbed him. Basil, airborne, calls them fooled. Silent Jim conjures the Captain with the ship's sails for wings, and seventeen fall charmed. When the rest rush Lumi, he turns into a winged, ghastly shape. Basil, out of spells, slaps the man in the lead with a skeletal hand, and he drops dead. The crowd scatters.

The dead man was a captain; Basil makes him talk. Was it worth it? *"It was a pretty good bar."* His ship was the Albatross. Lumi takes 19 silver and boots for Ted Two. The furniture comes aboard, and the Manticore shoves off from the Rock with none of the haulers aboard. Who else wears a rain cloud? What was the green one looking for in Lumi's mind? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
