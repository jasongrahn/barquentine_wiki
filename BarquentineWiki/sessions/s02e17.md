# 30-50 Feral Chickens

*S2E17 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E17 30-50 Feral Chickens.transcript.vtt`*

At Tiny's Tipsy, Basil says the ship's name one time too many, and the bar goes silent. Then: *clop, step. Clop, step.* Up the stairs comes Douglas, a mountain of a sea dog with a peg leg and a platinum hook he uses to drink straight from the pitcher. He knows the Manticore. It started life as the Ki-Rin, a creature that's *"a blessing plain and simple,"* and the fastest ship on the sea. It raced a dragon, lassoed to its neck, until the dragon fell from exhaustion. Its captain fell to an arrow. Her lover took the helm and renamed it for Vengeance. Eons later, an heir of that same dragon dragged it out of dry dock, and *"that's when the ship gained its"* sentience. And a manticore wears a human face for a reason: to lure you close.

Centaury 21 can't fix the ship, only bolt armor onto it, for 7,000 gold the crew doesn't have. So Basil tries a cheaper idea. One chicken, eldritch-blasted dead in the street. One live chicken, bought at market and bled on the poop deck. The ship's verdict, in Basil's head: *"no glory and no malice."* It wants a prize. A captured crew, with their captain for dessert. Room turns away. Lumi wonders aloud what a person would do, and tells Room about his old job: sorting the living from the dying at his father's celestial office, until his eyes started pouring black around his 16th birthday.

Basil tries to hang up on the voice in his head. He manages it, and the world tilts. For the first time in a year he's cold. So he tells Lumi the truth: he took the compass off a captain he killed, and the voice in it, the ship, taught him magic. It's his patron. The more he kills and plunders, the stronger they both get. The crew settle on a plan they can live with: hunt pirates and sea monsters, things that deserve it, and let the ship eat.

Below decks, the captain's quarters have been jammed since the ship rose. Basil and Room throw their weight into the door together. It crashes open, and they fall into blackness. Sunlight, seagulls, warm sea air: a stateroom straight out of the past, and a statuesque woman pulling back the curtains. She's Captain Welsh, and she wants to know why her ensigns are listening at her door. *"Now we've got a dragon to catch."* Who is Captain Welsh? Is this the Ki-Rin's past, or a trap? And will Basil and Room ever get out of her stateroom? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
