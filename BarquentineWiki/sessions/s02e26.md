# Down Next to a Clown

*S2E26 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E26 Down next to a clown.transcript.vtt`*

The Rock's market never sleeps; every hour of the day happens there at once. Mr. Catastrophe promised a friend here, someone who would look familiar. Then Room spots another loxodon in the crowd. It bolts, squeaking with every step. In a dead-end alley it waits in a feathered suit just like his own, returns his trunk greeting, and mirrors every step and word. Room: *"I don't think your name's Room."* It says it right back. Lumi tears a page in two so each Room can write down, in blood ink, the name of whoever is in Lumi's room. As the answers come out, the second Room is suddenly a clown.

Space clowns. Face paint that isn't paint, ray guns, big red shoes that squeak. Basil greases the alley under the wrestling pair, and three more open fire from the rooftops. Room swarms two with bugs, Basil knocks both off the roofs, and a fourth flees. Hit hard enough, a space clown pops like a balloon in a splash of acid. The last, the copy, cornered, asks for a parley, promises no funny business, and explains that it picked Room because he looked confused. Then it shocks itself and bursts all over Basil and Lumi.

The crew pocket noses, shoes and a cowboy hat, and a passing bureaucrat sends word of another infestation of space clowns. The words roll down the street, and shutters slam all along it. On the Rock everyone kills space clowns on sight and burns what they wear. Nobody enforces the law here; anyone out of line gets spaced. Room finds the Breathe Bar, a misty greenhouse, but its attendant has never seen another loxodon.

Back aboard, Room unrolls the map. Now that they've reached one stop, a dot appears for the next, and calligraphy names it: Eberron. Elder Abarat says they should not go. It is his home planet, ravaged by war. He hasn't been back in two hundred years, and *"as far as I know, nothing grows from glass."* The ship will need legs, armaments and armor. Where did the fourth clown go? Where is Mr. Catastrophe's friend? And what waits on a world of glass? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
