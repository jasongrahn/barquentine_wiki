# TreBlueChet

*S2E3 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E3 TreBlueChet.transcript.vtt`*

Room has a dream of flight, soaring up into the sky with no limits where he could go. Starting with Basil and the crew, then different people from different points of his life. As the dream progressed and people from his recent history came and went, he sees a portal to the industrial lands of his city-planet home. As he nears, the portal turns into an eye. Waking up, he evokes the words of his grandmother, "Baku come eat my dreams" and the oddness of the dream passes.

The escape plan takes shape. The team climb to the limits of the cell block getting them within 60 feet of the aperture. Basil casting flight on himself and Phthalos to carry Luminariel and Blue, and Room to shrinks down to pocket size after telling a story of his home, invoking the power of the shanty. The team rockets upward and into the fray…
