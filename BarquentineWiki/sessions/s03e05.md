# Shooting a Gift Horse in the Mouth

*S3E5 · recorded 2026-10-20 · source: `GMT20261020-011820_Recording.transcript.vtt`*

Ted finally had something of his own. So naturally, Lumi tried to kill it. Desperate to feed the cursed ship and get off this glass rock, he rang the bells of death over Ted's horse. The mane blazed gold. Lumi's own family sigil flared on its haunch. And through it came his mother's voice, scolding her son for *"shooting a gift horse in the mouth."* The horse nearly took his ear off on the way out. Back in his quarters, something was humming in his locker: his father's bronzed rib cage, bigger than he remembered. He put it on. It cinched tight and fused into his armor.

The plan, as always on this ship, was insane: get swallowed whole. Out on the glass, every step Lumi took rippled through the ground like a subwoofer you can't locate. The crew huddled in a gully. The cracks beneath them crumbled to sugar, and the worm's maw erupted around them. In the last instant before the jaws snapped shut, Mira uttered one word.

But worm didn't eat them. It carried them deep, spat them out without a scratch into a glowing cavern, and curled up to sleep. Down here, under all that glass, the planet was alive: a bioluminescent pond, an aquifer full of alien fish and insects, flowers in real dirt, tiny worms at the water's edge. Wonderful news, unless Room is right, and the glass is the only thing keeping Baku buried.

Then the Captain said the wrong thing out loud: if Mira wanted the ship so badly, she could have it. Something slipped away. And the ship, his patron, answered through the cavern's echoes for the whole crew to hear. It was hurt. It was hangry. And it knew exactly what it wanted for dinner: two captains fighting over it, *"and I get to eat the loser."*

Did the Captain just loosen his grip on his own ship? Is the worm healing this world, or digging up what was buried? And if Mira comes for the Manticore… who ends up the loser? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
