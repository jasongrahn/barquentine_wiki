# It Really Ties It Together

*S3E6 · recorded 2026-11-03 · source: `GMT20261104-021831_Recording.transcript.vtt`*

Fifteen hours. That's how long the worm's tunnels held the crew before they clawed back into the light, on a stretch of glass nobody recognized. Two suns overhead. Lumi's uncanny sense of north. Eight more hours of marching. And finally: the Manticore.

Empty.

*"Oh yeah, she came back,"* the ship sulked. *"For sure."*

Alistair's tools lay where they'd fallen. Lumi's missing hairbrush lay in the dust. Gouges were ripped across the glass. Something *big* came through here. Room pressed his trunk to the ground and caught the scent: old. Mahogany. Leather-bound books. Mira. She took everything. The crew. The horses. Victoria's chest, and the key inside it. The ancient coins.

Even. The. Credenza.

So, back to the hut. The Captain kicked the bedroom door, and the door kicked back, straight into his face. He tore the room apart and ripped up the woven rug, and there it was: a trapdoor. Below it, a shaft of polished glass plunged into the dark, far too wide for any old woman. Room hushed their steps. The Captain turned them invisible. Three heroes, holding hands, slid down into the throat of the world, past Warforged sealed in the walls. Every one of them placed to *watch*.

At the bottom was a cavern of mirrors, and her collection. Lock, in crystal up to his mouth. Stock. Barrel. Cletus. Robert. Ted. Sirvellica, jaw sealed shut. Billow, curled in a pillar no bigger than a jar. The Captain's own parents. The horses, hooves fused to the floor. The whole crew, frozen in glass. Elder Abarat, set at the center like a centerpiece.

And against the wall… the credenza. It really does tie the room together.

Then Mira walked right into the invisible Captain. She knew. The act dropped. She thanked them for the reminder: taking beats asking. And she began to *grow*. Her attention turned to the Captain and to his ancestors, the banished Giff who fought to keep this world. *"How they fought. How they screamed. How delicious they were."*

When she stopped growing, there was no old woman left.

Only a dragon.

Three exhausted heroes. A whole crew trapped in glass. One dragon. Can they get everyone out? What else did Mira take from the Giff, and where is she hiding it? And the oldest plan this crew has ever had (get swallowed, fight your way out): can it work *one more time*?

Find out this time, on Barquentine!
