# It Really Ties It Together

*S3E6 · recorded 2026-11-03 · source: `GMT20261104-021831_Recording.transcript.vtt`*

The worm's tunnels run everywhere, and they lead nowhere you'd want to go. It took the crew fifteen hours to claw their way back to the light. They surfaced on some stretch of glass nobody recognized, steered by two suns and Lumi's uncanny sense of north, then marched for eight more hours straight back to the Manticore. The ship was waiting, sulking. Yes, the old woman had come back. No, nobody was left. Alistair's tools lay where they'd fallen. Lumi's missing hairbrush lay in the dust. Long gouges ran across the glass. Something big had come through here. Room put his trunk to the ground and smelled something old, mixed with mahogany and leather-bound books: Mira's house. And she'd taken everything. The crew, the horses, Victoria's chest with the key inside, the ancient coins. Even the credenza.

So they went back to the hut. The Captain kicked the bedroom door open, and it bounced straight back into his face. He tore the room apart anyway, and under the woven rug he found a trapdoor. He knew it. Below it, a shaft of polished glass dropped steeply away, wide enough for something far bigger than an old woman. Room hushed their steps, the Captain turned them invisible, and the three of them slid down holding hands so they wouldn't lose each other. They passed Warforged sealed in the walls, every one of them turned to watch.

At the bottom lay a mirrored cavern, and off to one side, her collection. Lock was sealed in crystal up to his mouth. Then Stock and Barrel, Cletus, Robert, and Ted. Sirvellica's jaw was sealed shut. Billow was curled in a pillar no bigger than a jar. Elder Abarat sat at the center like a centerpiece. The Captain's parents were there too, and the horses, hooves fused to the floor. The whole crew, encased. And against the wall stood the credenza. It really tied the room together.

Then Mira walked right into the invisible Captain. She already knew they were there. She dropped the act and thanked them for reminding her that taking beats asking. As she grew, she turned her attention to the Captain and his ancestors, the banished Giff who'd fought to keep this world: *"How they fought. How they screamed. How delicious they were."* Then she was done growing. Standing over the crew was a dragon.

Can three exhausted heroes free a whole crew from glass with a dragon standing over them? What else did Mira take from the Giff, and where is she keeping it? And will the oldest plan this crew has ever had work one more time: let her swallow them and fight their way out? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
