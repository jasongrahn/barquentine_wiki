# Definitely Won't Be a Problem Later

*S2E8 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E8 Definitely won't be a problem later..transcript.vtt`*

Room had a nervous dream about wasting away with visions of his home. His uncle wearing war tusks, his home world on fire, and a monstrous, destructive, menacing creature beckoning for him to come home. He calls out "baku, come eat my dreams" and the visions slide away. Above deck, the inky moon returns to normal and Basil teaches Lumi some ship terms and how to pilot the vessel.

Basil takes a dip to communicate w/ his patron, diving deep into the water. He's given yet another cryptic message on how to proceed, along with a request for snacks… Later while detecting the magic of the compass, he learns that while a normal compass divines "north", these compasses devine "the way forward."

During team discussion on how to either subdue Phthalos or try to get him on their side, Basil dropped why he has a sense of urgency to find this ship.. that *his* parents are looking down legal troubles that might have them at the end of the hangman's noose. At the same time, Blue reveals that there's a *third* plasmoid out there searching for him.

Failing his meditation, Blue shatters his bucket and approaches Phthalos. Through their argument, he asks Blue "Would you serve me?" His response "No, I dont think I would." leaves us in tension as the crew sail further out to sea…
