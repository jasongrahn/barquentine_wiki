# Should've Used the Field Trip Rope

*S2E18 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E18 Should've used the Field Trip Rope.transcript.vtt`*

Behind the captain's door, it's the Ki-Rin a thousand years ago. Cherry and mahogany, gold inlay, a galley stocked like a king's. Basil and Room look down at human hands, with far too many fingers, and they're just two deckhands in a memory. On the map, the ship's course runs back to an archipelago called Old Zealand. Up on deck, the figurehead is no rotted wreck. It's a gleaming Ki-Rin, half dragon, half unicorn, and Captain Welsh stands on it, shouting a challenge into the sea. Tomorrow at dawn, a race for a young blue dragon's hoard. And if the ship loses? Simple, she says: *"he'll crash the ship and eat all of us."*

Back in the present, Lumi finally reads the mousekin's two scraps of paper. One word each. *Stupid mouse.* Then he finds a new mission: a person's blood instead of a chicken's. A cat-whiskered merchant takes his gold piece and hands him a fish. The jailhouse answers to the Cofishioner, a fish person in an aquarium, and he won't lease out prisoners: for all he knows, Lumi would be chopping off their hands. Back at the ship, an elf is already on deck. He calls himself Captain Bhurgher, of the flagship of an elven fleet. He sneers at the ship, wipes its mold on Lumi's cloak, and barges into an empty room looking for the captain. Lumi strikes first. The elf fights him back to the stairs, then storms off, promising to speak with Lumi's captain and have his head.

Dawn, in the past. Full gale, every spellcaster pushing the wind, and the dragon weaving under the hull. It surfaces just to look at Captain Welsh: *"this is fun for me, you are but toys."* The signal comes. Room conjures a blue dragon twin to shoulder it off its line, and Basil's giant magic hand grabs the very tip of its tail. Ballistae fire ropes. A lasso drops from the crow's nest. The dragon dives, and the ship launches. That night, the divers bring up the hoard, and Captain Welsh gives "Stevie" and "Rex" a private feast and a chest of 600 platinum. When Room opens the door, the stateroom is rotten again.

For Lumi, they were gone 2 hours and 45 minutes. For them, about two days. The coins are from the House of Welsh of Old Zealand, a thousand years old and in mint condition, and Lumi wants them protected at all costs. The captain's quarters are still jammed. A few piers down sits King Bhurgher's ship, and Basil has a hunch about who "Captain Bhurgher" really is. What else lives behind that door? Did two deckhands just change the Ki-Rin's history? And what does a very short, very snobby captain do with a declaration of war? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
