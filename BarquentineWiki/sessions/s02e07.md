# Unexpected and Unappreciated

*S2E7 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E7 Unexpected and Unappreciated Part 1.transcript.vtt` and `… Part 2.transcript.vtt`*

The crew is on their way!

Well, kind of.

With the wind at their backs and no chase in sight, Basil checked his compass for a heading - as the needle spun wildly, he declared a heading while Luminariel dropped mega-lore by stating his preferred short-hand name: Lumi.

A follow-up check to the compass gives a more stable direction.. And it leads Basil directly toward Phthalos. As Basil investigates, the rest of the crew independently decide it's a great time for a nap.

As they slept, the ship crashed into a small shipwreck on a sandbar. Without too much damage to the hull, they find a few items left aboard the derelict vessel while Lumi assumed conspiracy alongside Basils advice they should be prepared to be attacked by ghosts.

  - A few scrolls
  - A pair of glasses
  - A jug
  - And a breastplate that appears as new as the day it was made

Flexing some new-found spellpower, Basil waterbends the ship off the sandbar and they're again out to sea. As they get aboard the ship, Basil attempts to communicate with his patron, receiving cryptic messages in return.. "The Ki-Rin hasn't been here for some time." and in response to "my compass isn't working", he receives "Compass functions perfectly, but you might not be the right man for the job."

As with everything related to the ship, Phthalos also gives cryptic answers around how he intends to find the ship. After a bit of back and forth, Lumi casts Huddle and brings the group together for more conspiracy… What if the ships they both are after are the same ship?

Blue finishes with… "Whatever we do, we need to keep Phthalos alive."
