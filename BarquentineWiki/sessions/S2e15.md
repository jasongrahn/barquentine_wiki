## Summary

After the heat surrounding the gnome depot murders drops, [[Large Luigi]] is cleared of suspicion due to an alibi from a supply meeting. The party turns attention to recruiting a new crew over the following two weeks. A message arrives for [[Room]] from his grandmother. Trouble looms, and the ship itself may exist in a dimension of its own.

## Key Events
- [[Large Luigi]] notes that business has been booming ever since someone killed the gnomes from the [[Gnome Depot]] pop-up shop
- [[Large Luigi]] claims he was in a supply meeting and has proof, clearing him of suspicion
- The party spends the next two weeks focused on crew recruitment
- [[Room]] receives a message back from his grandmother: *"My sweet baby boy, little cherub peach bottom"*
- A lore note is established: all [[Loxodon]] instinctively know the way home in their soul
- The party is described as sleeping on the ship, far from the ship — referred to as **THE SHIP DIMENSION**
- Someone warns: *"We are all in trouble."*
