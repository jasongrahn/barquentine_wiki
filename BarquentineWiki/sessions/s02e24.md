# Three on a Ship's Hook

*S2E24 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E24 Three on a ship's hook.transcript.vtt`*

The moth-shaped ship is a star moth, and Basil knows the design: astral elves, self-appointed police of wild space. Ten minutes out, he remembers his contraband. One Terillian corn cob, which never goes bad and is a little hallucinogenic. It goes back in his holster. Basil taps the ship, and the ship chuckles. It is still hurt, broken bones and scabbed wounds, and it is in a hurry to heal.

So the plan is a trap. Robert slips over the side, the star moth comes in from above, and three warriors leap across, each in their own bubble of air. Basil tries to talk their captain into looking over the rail for smugglers who aren't there. The elf has a better name for the smuggler: Mr. Cream, a Giff, and Basil is a Giff too. *"Are you profiling me?"* He is. Basil goes for a shove, and it's a fight.

It isn't much of one. A whirling cloud of scalding gyoza spirits kills the elf captain, and his blood doesn't splatter: it soaks into the deck, and the rotten planks turn to fresh timber. A voice in Basil's head calls him captain, and his rod of the pact keeper grows stronger. When Basil drops the second warrior, the ship heals completely, up the mast but not the sails, right up to the figurehead, where it stops. The last elf leaps into wild space, the ballistae bring him down, and Lumi drags the body to his cabin. *"See you guys later."*

Basil tells the crew there are definitely no loose ends, and they cheer. Then he learns the rules. Violent death aboard heals the ship, and it loved this: a boarding party baited and overwhelmed. Something still blocks the figurehead. And when one captain comes to do violence to another, that is grounds for deciding who owns the ship next. Which way did the star moth run? What is blocking the figurehead? And what does Lumi want with the last elf's body? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
