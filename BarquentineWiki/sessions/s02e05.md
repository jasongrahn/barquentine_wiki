# It's Called Pacifism, Everyone!

*S2E5 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E5 , It's called Pacifism, everyone!.transcript.vtt`*

The team push down the Monolith and commandeer a ship. As the battle carries on, Phthalos's attack seems to pull the soul of his victim into pulled down into a fiery chasm that closes behind it.

Luminariel gives a rousing speech that gives Blue the motivation he needs to stun-locks a mage and throw him overboard.

In a moment of chaos, Luminariel smashes the ship against it's neighbor, throwing everyone off foot, giving Room the misbalance he needs to use the power of the shanty to dash the captain's head against a cannon and throws him overboard to drown.

"Ted the bosun" flees from Phthalos, while Basil and Blue distract the wizards from being able to keep their arcane chain from staying latched to the monolith. The ship comes untethered, and the monolith crashes into the ocean. As it does, the sky changes into a tar-like black with the moon growing an eerie smile as the ship heads out to sea.
