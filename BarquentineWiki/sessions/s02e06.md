# 2 Hours Until Sunrise

*S2E6 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E6 2 hours until sunrise.transcript.vtt`*

The Crew have set sail away from the monolith.

Ted the sailor stayed on board and after a few veiled threats, took to the crows nest as everyone divvied up ships duties. Blue manned the helm, while Luminariel chummed the waters with the blood and bodies remaining on deck.

Going through his pilfered inventory, Room finds a healing potion, an invisibility potion, a dancing scimitar, and a spell scroll for Insect Plague. After a bit of trading, Rooms keeps the scimitar and gives the warhammer to Luminariel.

While Phthalos snacks on a body, Luminariel learns it's the green plasmoid's anchor that pulled the soul from the body into the lower planes.

Blue & Luminariel discuss the letter Blue brought from home. Luminariel identifies the script on the letter as Abyssal. Basil gets the idea to summon a demon to help read the letter. After some preparation to banish, and convincing Ted with a tasty sandwich, Basil summons an incubus to help read the letter.

After sucking down some of Ted's life force, the demon called "Seggxy" "Mr Hero" or "Walk of Shame" reads the letter…

Wisdom is your greatest weapon. Embrace the chaos and learn from it. In the depths of the abyss, clarity can be found through understanding and insight. Seek out your siblings, for their strength and faith are essential to achieving true balance. You must unify them to bring harmony to all.

With the ship underway, Phthalos checks a perfectly crafted, monstrously ornate compass… Looks up, and states "Must not be working right now." then puts it back in his pocket.
