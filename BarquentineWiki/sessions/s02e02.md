# Still Just a Blob in a Cage

*S2E2 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E2.transcript.vtt`*

The Monolith keeps no guards. It doesn't need them. A tone sounds, the cell doors open, and when it sounds again every prisoner walks back inside on their own. Twenty-four hours at the bottom of the tower, and Lumi has stopped pretending he can wait it out. He wants out, and he wants this crew. So he tells them what the Soul Gaze showed him. Phthalos is dead set on a ship of bones, and once he has it, *"he will murder us."* Phthalos doesn't argue the point.

Trust costs something down here, so Lumi pays for it. He tells them about the bronzed torso in his parents' chest. Then he turns the Soul Gaze on Blue, and it works both ways. Lumi finds question marks, a red plasmoid called Hierophant Vermilion, and a secret Blue hasn't told anybody. Blue gets one back: Lumi's parents didn't just encourage him to see the world. They told him to leave.

Then the bones went missing. The pile is picked clean and the latrine corner never fills, so something is carrying the waste away. Lumi follows three-eyed rats to find out what, and the floor answers with carrion beetles and swarms of scarabs. Room, the pacifist, tries diplomacy. The bugs click back *"you food."* One scarab burrows into his leg, and Room stops being a pacifist. He grabs it, rips its head off and keeps the head as a spell focus. Blue spins through the swarms, and Lumi rings Toll the Dead for his first non-human kill. Phthalos rages with a four-foot femur, pulps the last beetle, bites its head and walks back to his cell. Lumi watches and isn't impressed. For all that rage, Phthalos is *"still just a blob in a cage."*

The beetles left tracks, and the tracks lead up to the aperture. Basil flies with Room in tow. Past the last cell block, past the last stairs, up into the mirrored shaft, close enough to see guards through the opening. Close enough for the guards to see a hippo in an orange jumpsuit. A shout booms down, the flight is dispelled, and they fall. Feather Fall catches them, and a voice makes the rules clear: *"This is your one warning."* Each of them has used theirs. How many more chances does the Monolith hand out? Is Blue's secret safe with Lumi? And what does Phthalos get once the crew have served their purpose? Find out next time, on Barquentine!
