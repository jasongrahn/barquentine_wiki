# The Wind in Your Hair

*S2E23 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E23 The wind in your hair.transcript.vtt`*

They wake on the roof of the Elven Rodeo. Basil still wears his cloak of the bat, Lumi still clutches the parchment, and Room remembers none of it. Not every one of Mr. Catastrophe's simulacra survived the night: where some stood, there are puddles of water and piles of mashed potatoes. The crew recall what the sludge said as it ate the dream: feed me your dream. Room's grandmother taught him the reverse, a charm against nightmares. Mr. Catastrophe has a theory. If every loxodon believes in a thing that eats dreams, maybe belief has willed it into being, and maybe it's stopped being kind.

The recipe for sweet honeyfish moon cakes has a map on the back, a chain of hops through wild space that leads Room home. The first stop is a hub in the shadow of the moon where you can buy anything: the Rock. Mr. Catastrophe has a friend up there. You'll know him when you see him. He's already been paid, too: while they slept, he copied the magic out of their rod, and boy howdy, is he going to make a mint.

At the ship, Basil reaches out to the Manticore and gets the silent treatment. This ship mends only with blood shed in battle. Sirvellica pours the gold, Elder Abarat waves the rod, and the bone throne welds to the deck as energy pulses out like veins to the mast and the figurehead. Basil sits, gives up his pearl to make room, and suddenly the wind in the sails, the water at the waterline and the salt of the bay are all his own body.

He wants to fly. The ship jumps, and King Bhurgher shakes his fist from the food court below as the Manticore climbs out of the fog of Bral. Lumi asks what it feels like. *"I am not the captain. I am the ship."* A bubble of air forms around the hull and the stars open up. Then something slows them, and off the bow a second vessel appears, shaped like a moth. Who is Mr. Catastrophe's friend on the Rock? What has willed the dream-eater into being? And what does the ship want, now that it has wings? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
