# Hot Hands

*S2E20 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E20 Hot Hands.whisper.vtt` (Whisper transcript of the recording; YouTube has no captions)*

Aboard the Raft, President Mario pulls the sheet off his table: a model of the Beak. Fourteen beds plus the suite, so maybe fifteen vampirates of the Red Flag Fleet, who answer to Dread Captain Yi. Get into the bathing chambers, swim to the bottom of the pool, and the laboratory waits below. The recipe is the prize, because nobody can recreate it from memory. It pays two thousand gold for the job and two hundred more per crate of moon cakes, plus a value add: a brand-new cloak of the bat for each of them. Room has to talk through his spells. A candle flame floats over the map, from the storage room to the dock and back, while two thin lines of dust run from the back door to the pool. When the talk turns to burning the whole place down, the paper in Lumi's pocket heats up again. Lumi has a theory. The mug at the tavern got hot, too. *"New hot hands, I guess."* (Room heated that mug.)

The Raft drops them behind a ridge with one hour on the clock. The compound glows yellow and rings with drunken shanties, two guards patrol the path, and everyone is awake. They forgot to ask what Periwinkle looks like. Basil blows a fog cloud through his compasses, and the cloaks lift them onto the roof just as a guard rounds the corner, ornate dagger drawn, sure he heard something. His partner shrugs: *"It's all that cheese you've been eating."* Room conjures the smell of cheese. The vampirates wander off, wistful.

Through the skylight is the suite: a pelt rug, tanning oil, a box of Speedos. Down the hall, a vampirate snores under a pistol. Lumi slips past. Basil rolls a disaster, but Room watches the dream out of order. He sees the sleeper wake before the noise even happens. Basil stubs his toe, but his foot is already over the threshold, and the yelp never comes. *Thank you, Baku.* In the bath chamber a vampirate soaks with his head underwater and his legs over the tub, and Lumi pockets the platinum hook lying on the floor, the kind that replaces a hand. Basil finds a raised tile at the bottom of the pool. They surface outside the laboratory, and Lumi is drenched and irritated.

A steel door. Two dials, too far apart for one creature to reach. A maze of red lasers that look gray to them. Room turns Lumi into a cat; the lasers burn him back to himself, and Room's gust of wind blows him the rest of the way. Then Basil becomes a rat, since there's no mouse to be had, and comes out singed. With his ear to the wall, Basil hears gears and keys fall into place and fall out again. Turn both dials together. They start by lining them up. Whose hand did that hook replace? Where is Periwinkle? And is that heat really coming from Lumi? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
