# Hi I'm Ted

*S2E15 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E15 Hi I'm Ted.transcript.vtt`*

Cyan is gone, and the crew is down to three. Two weeks on Bral to shop, recruit and breathe. Basil wakes to Billow the armadillo kneading his chest. Lumi learns some armadillos carry leprosy. And Ted, who once thought this crew would kill him, then eat him, then let a demon drink his soul, signs on for good: if they feed him, pay for the hotels and get him his gear, *"then I'm on board."* By evening his catchphrase is ringing through the Elven Rodeo: *"Hi, I'm Ted."*

Downstairs, in his laboratory, Elder Abarat lays out the plan. The rod from the bottom of the ocean can make the ship fly: set a big throne chair on deck, ring it with 600 platinum, say the words. He looks at Lumi like he's seen a ghost. Someone who looked a lot like him, in a terrible war, made a deal and became the very first warforged. Then Abarat confesses his own war work: he built the very first mimic. He also built Lucy, and Phthalos made him hand her over by beating him with his own fist. When the crew explain how Blue swallowed Phthalos and became Cyan, Abarat mutters that someone's research is going too far, and won't say whose.

The shopping spree. The mousekin who runs Bards and Nobles has no book for Lumi's question about dismembered, bronzed bodies, only a list: warlock pacts, sacrifices to deities, stealing a soul so the body can never be raised. Then he presses two scraps of paper into Lumi's hand and won't say what's on them. *Don't read them aloud. Now get out.* Large Luigi swears he didn't kill the Gnome Depot gnomes, hands Basil his special order (a shrunken, gilded rib cage on a keychain) and takes a commission for a bone throne, built from his own pit of bones, for 600 gold.

At Furrizon Wireless, Room spends 100 gold of his own on a message home: *I am lost on the planet Toril. Can you help me get home? Love you, miss you.* The answer lands like a meteor in his mind. His grandmother, crying, in ceremonial paint: *my sweet baby boy.* All loxodon know the way home is in their soul. Then: *"we are all in trouble."* Room's family needs him. Basil's parents need him. The bill is enormous, the scraps are unread, and Ted has just found out you can gamble at the Elven Rodeo. What did the mousekin write down? What trouble has found Room's people? And can the crew pay for any of it? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
