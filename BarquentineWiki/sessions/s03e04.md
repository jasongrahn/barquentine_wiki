# Actually, This Is Quite Useful

*S3E4 · recorded 2026-09-30 · source: `GMT20260930-011150_Recording.transcript.vtt`* · recap: DM's S3e5 intro (Google Doc "Barquentine S3 notes")

Plan A died the way most plans on this ship do. To pin the worm in place, the crew needed the immovable rolling pin triggered from a safe distance. Mage Hand could hold it but couldn't press the button, so Lumi conjured his spiritual gyoza and dropped it from above. It found the button. Then it kept going, and the Suppernova prize came out flat.

Out of better ideas, the crew followed Mira home before heading underground, and the Captain started noticing things. Walls carved like a captain's quarters. A table and chairs of Giff make. While Mira excused herself to the back room, a Ghostly Gaze through the wall found that room empty. She came back changed and brushed off, with a blunderbuss on her back stamped three generations old. Giff, too.

The crew chose a borehole at a gentle slope as the two suns set, and walked down with their light breaking a thousand ways through the glass. Deeper, the walls showed layers, blast on blast, each one glassing the last, and the worm had cut through every one of them without slowing. A buried basement opened in the tunnel wall, and in it were pieces of Elder Abarat's people, entombed close enough to the surface to see. Then the crunch under Lumi's feet changed. Not glass. Dirt. After that, nothing new. The tunnel just kept going, exactly as Mira had warned. *"That's kind of why I've never really tried."*

Back aboard, the Robe of Useful Items had its own ideas about what the party needed: three more ladders, a scroll of Spirit Guardians, a silver coffer, a window that turns any wall no thicker than itself into a way through; and two full riding horses complete with saddle bags, one of which now belongs to Ted, who hugged Lumi for it. Alistair spent the afternoon calling the Manticore cursed to his son's face, but he took one look at the discarded ladders and changed his tune: *"Actually, this is quite useful."*

Then every clue went on the table, and none of them pointed anywhere new. The creature from Elder Abarat's tale came first. The Warforged fled. The planet was glassed. Lumi still believes the worm belongs here, but every way they've looked at the mystery says *kill it*. Mira hasn't pushed. She only made sure they understood the price.

Is the worm healing this world, or digging something back up? What is a three-generation-old Giff blunderbuss doing strapped to Mira's back? And if the crew kills the only thing eating the glass, is there anyone or anything waiting for it to stop?

**Find out this time, on Barquentine!**
