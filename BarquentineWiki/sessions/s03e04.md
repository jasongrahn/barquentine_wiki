# Actually, This Is Quite Useful

*S3E4 · recorded 2026-09-30 · source: `GMT20260930-011150_Recording.transcript.vtt`*

Plan A was simple: pin the worm in place with an immovable rolling pin. The hard part was pressing the button from a safe distance. A watery spectral hand held the pin upright. Lumi conjured a gyoza made of glass, lifted it high, and brought it down on the button. It hit. Then it crushed the trophy from the Flotilla's cooking contest flat. So much for Plan A.

So Mira took them home. Her hut had once been a captain's quarters, with ship-carved walls and a table and chairs of Giff make. She excused herself to the bedroom. The Captain's eyes went deep-sea blue, and he looked straight through the wall. The room was empty. When Mira came back she was in a fresh outfit, brushing herself off, with a Giff blunderbuss strapped to her back, marked three generations old. She'd never fired it at the worm. Not without backup.

As the two suns set, the crew walked down a gently sloping borehole while the light above them broke into a thousand colors through the glass. The walls held the planet's history in strata: an explosion, a layer of glass, another explosion, another layer. The worm had tunneled through every one of them. A buried basement opened in the wall, with pieces of Warforged entombed close enough to see. Then the crunch under Lumi's feet changed: dirt. After that, nothing. Just more tunnel, exactly as Mira had warned.

Back aboard and out of ideas, Lumi emptied his mother's robe of useful items. Out came a spell scroll, a silver coffer, a window that was (the Admiral insisted) just a window, three more ladders, and two horses with saddlebags. One horse went to Ted, who hugged him for it. Alistair, who had spent the afternoon calling the ship cursed, took one look at a discarded ladder: *"Actually, this is quite useful."* Then every clue went on the table, and none of them pointed anywhere new. But the ship had been listening, and it liked where Lumi's jokes about Ted's horse were heading: *"you're gonna kill his horse and feed it to me? A tier right there, dude."*

Is the worm the planet's killer or its cure? Where did Mira go when she left that empty room? And how far will Lumi go to feed a cursed ship? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
