# Don't Go in My Room!

*S2E25 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E25 Don't go in my room!.transcript.vtt`*

On the way to the Rock, a ship slides out from behind an asteroid: an enormous spiral shell, long tentacles waving in no current at all. One of Basil's rules of space is that tentacles are bad. Ted is volunteered to jump first, with a rope and a spare air bubble. *"I'm beginning to think that you guys don't know what you're doing."* He lands, waves, and gives a thumbs up.

It's a nautiloid, the kind of ship mind flayers run. Its doors are fleshy membranes. In the captain's chair sits a headless purple corpse, and when Basil disturbs it, its maggots rise as a swarm until he freezes them into space. The crew take a scroll, a small stone of protection and eighty rounds of siege ammunition. Further in lies a Githyanki, one of the people who hunt mind flayers. Two headless bodies and a hunter: somebody took trophies. They leave before they find anything still alive.

The Rock is a city clinging like barnacles to a turning asteroid. While Basil and Room try a bar full of centaurs, Lumi stays aboard with the blanketed shape in his cabin. He draws a sigil in Ted's blood on the dead elf's forehead, names it Ted Two, and sets it to guard the chest of ancient coins. He asks if it remembers its old name. It doesn't answer.

Behind an antler in Dust and Echoes is the Hush, a velvet room where they deal in memories, names, favors, contracts and whispers. In a back booth waits a thin, apelike figure dressed like a retired undertaker: the Ledger. Basil wants to know where Mr. Cream and his parents are. *"I need one of your names."* Basil points out he has already given his first name. The Ledger accepts it, and a cold mist crawls into his lungs and back out. Mr. Cream is bound for the Giff Flotilla, and Basil's parents are already there, awaiting trial. If it goes wrong, they end up on the Marble, a glassed planet where the Giff banish their own. What did the Ledger take? What waits on the Marble? And what is Ted Two guarding in Lumi's room? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
