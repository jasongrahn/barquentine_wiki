# Snatch Interlude

*S2E29 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E29 Snatch interlude.transcript.vtt`*

With the Captain's player away, the table takes a side adventure: Room's player is Lock, Lumi's player is Stock, and the DM runs Barrel. Lumi's orders: a general store, not the bar where the crew met the Ledger, and maybe buy some generals too. Stock spots an official-looking man in a fancy suit, who, cornered, buys them coffee and explains generals. Barrel is the general of walking, Lock of stabbing, Stock of thinking. Lock spikes his coffee with a whole flask. Their host is a general of trading, and his name is Theodore. They walk off with the cafe's buckets, which the barista keeps for customers who need to puke.

Theodore leaves them at the Captain's Quarters, a lounge where captains drink, trade and hire crew. But a bar sells by the cup, and they need barrels. Stock and Barrel go in as proper captains; Lock cases the back. A cart pulls into the alley with ten barrels: whiskey, wine, ale and a dwarven ale said to make people go blind. Lock throws the driver's clipboard, then pitches him: he's starting his own operation and will double the pay. *"My friend, you are the general of plans."* They load full barrels back on the cart as "empties."

Meanwhile Stock is Captain Stock of the Cantamore, with a thimble of dwarven ale to sample. Barrel, "Captain Ted", grabs another captain's hundred-gold cheese. Their captain-versus-co-captain row wins the room, and the bar cheers its new captains. Stock tells them his ship heals itself on the blood of the dead, and that a crewmate with black-hole eyes may have raised a dead man.

By then, Lock has slipped in and opened the till. His signal is the clipboard, thrown into a watcher's nose. Someone shouts robbery, and a knife sinks into Lock's shoulder. Stock tackles Lock as the robber and vows to feed him to the Manticore. Barrel runs out the back with the register. The proprietor puts a pistol to Stock's head. Stock warns that they'd only be raised and come back to feed him to the ship, while Lock picks his teeth with a bigger knife. The proprietor gives in: *"Fine, I'll let you walk to the Manticore and we will all follow you."* How much booze makes it home? Who comes with it? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
