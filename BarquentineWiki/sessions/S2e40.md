## Summary

[[Basil|the Captain]] and [[Lumi]] exhaust a series of increasingly impractical escape schemes aboard the [[Manticore]] while [[Room]] works his way into the confidence of [[Alistair]] and [[Victoria]] from his brig cell, smuggling out a diplomatic note to the bone ship. [[Basil|the Captain]] descends alone to confront [[Geronimo]], who has shed his old name and any old loyalty, and uses [[Basil|the Captain]]'s own extracted intelligence report against him. A last-ditch appeal through a Minor Illusion of a toy cannon fails to move [[Geronimo]], who departs to arrange [[Alistair]] and [[Victoria]]'s banishment. [[Basil|the Captain]] and [[Lumi]] are arrested and marched into the brig — where they find [[Room]], [[Alistair]], and [[Victoria]] already waiting. Their weapons and spells remain, but the window is closing fast.

## Key Events
- [[Basil|the Captain]] and [[Lumi]] cycle through and discard multiple escape plans: zombies from a morgue ship, explosions, guard uniforms, space whales
- [[Room]] spends the night in pleasant conversation with [[Alistair]] and [[Victoria]], securing parchment, ink, and quill from a charmed brig guard
- [[Room]] sends a folded note addressed to the captain of "the fucked-up bone ship" via a dispatched runner
- [[Room]] tests [[Druidcraft]] between the bars; [[Victoria]] catches him and questions him; [[Room]] lies, claiming it is just a trick — a little wind
- [[Victoria]] asks about the captain being one of her offspring; [[Room]] lies; [[Victoria]] accepts it in a tone that signals suspicion
- [[Silent Jim]] has left a farewell note aboard the [[Manticore]]: *"The Astral Sea is where I needed to go. Thank you for bringing me home."* [[Basil|the Captain]] folds it into his coat and instructs [[Ted]] to find a new navigator
- [[Lumi]] distracts two guards with a fabricated story about free gunpowder from [[King Bührgher]]'s victory celebration, creating an opening
- [[Basil|the Captain]] descends below deck alone to confront [[Geronimo]], who appears in full tribunal regalia and states he no longer has a name
- [[Geronimo]] argues that [[Alistair]] and [[Victoria]]'s weapon could save Giff lives wasted on pointless expeditions and demands the artifact
- [[Geronimo]] produces [[Basil|the Captain]]'s own extracted intelligence report and reads it back as a formal charge — the weapon was secret, the admission is on record
- [[Basil|the Captain]] conjures a Minor Illusion of a 1/15th-scale cannon on [[Geronimo]]'s desk and appeals to shared childhood memory
- [[Geronimo]] is unmoved and departs to prepare [[Alistair]] and [[Victoria]]'s banishment, designating [[Lumi]] for punishment as well
- [[Basil|the Captain]] and [[Lumi]] are escorted by six guards into the brig, where they find [[Room]], [[Alistair]], and [[Victoria]]

## GM Notes
The confrontation with [[Geronimo]] was the emotional centrepiece of the session — his use of [[Basil|the Captain]]'s own intelligence report as a formal sentence is a clean narrative inversion. [[Victoria]] clocking [[Room]]'s Druidcraft and accepting his lie "in the motherly tone that showed her suspicion" flags her as a character who should not be underestimated. The Minor Illusion toy cannon beat is a strong character moment for [[Basil|the Captain]] — worth noting that it failed entirely. The party is now fully imprisoned with weapons still on them, setting up a brig breakout as the immediate next scene.
