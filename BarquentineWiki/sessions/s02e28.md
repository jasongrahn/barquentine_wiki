# Quarterbastard

*S2E28 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E28 Quarterbastard.transcript.vtt`*

Lumi stays aboard with important housekeeping. Ted Two is told to sit and touch nothing, so he hovers in a squat above the chair. Lock, Stock and Barrel come back early, drunk, kicked out of a bar, with no booze for the crew. Lumi gives them a sobriety test, pricks their palms for his inkwell, and sends them off to a general store. On the pier they bump into three frog-like humanoids, each a different color, who look up and ask Lumi about the ship's Giff captain. Then, as Lumi reads alone on deck, a voice in his head: *"I am the ship, my dude."* It thinks raising the dead is rad. Keep it going. Call it Manny.

Basil and Room return. The frog folk's names can't be pronounced by a human tongue. They want passage to the Astral Plane and pay from a webbed handful of gems, diamonds among them: half now, fifty thousand gold in gems, and as much again on arrival. Asked about dietary restrictions, the green one answers *"absolutely none"* with a glint in his eye, while the others laugh. Then the blue one pulls a handrail off the ship and throws it into space.

Basil and Lumi are sure the passengers mean to kill them and eat them. The scraps in Lumi's pocket run hot at murder talk. Sirvellica tries to say Basil's name, and her mouth goes dry as sand. When Lumi says he'd rather the crew didn't die, the scraps turn cold, and he lays out the whole story of the mousekin's words, which he won't read aloud. Manny suggests Lumi should be captain. Lumi says that sounds terrible. Room got no murder vibes at all. In the hold, the green one tells him they are bound for the plane of law, hunted by robots. Through the helm, Basil watches the blue one pick his teeth with a bone hook jutting from his hand.

Lock, Stock and Barrel roll in barrels from the back of a bar. Lumi opens his door on Ted Two: drink any more, and they'll be Ted Three, Four and Five. *"Loud and clear. Poor bastard."* What do the frog folk really want? What hunts them? And what does the ship see in Lumi? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
