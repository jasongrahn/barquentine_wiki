# This Is No Big Deal, Right

*S2E21 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E21 This is no big deal, right.transcript.vtt`*

Two dials, a wall of lasers, and Periwinkle nowhere to be found. Room's locate creature turns up no dohwar within a thousand feet. Basil knocks politely, and no one answers. Then he puts his ear to the wall and hears the tumblers. Turned one at a time, the dials reset each other. Wound back to the stops and turned together, both catch. The lasers die and the steel door swings open.

Beyond the door is a bakery laboratory: flour sacks, long worktables, a huge oven and dozens of faucets, all eerily silent. The door snaps shut behind them. The oven roars to life, every faucet overflows, and scalding steam fills the room while the water climbs toward their waists. There's no monster. The room is the trap. Lumi's theory: bake something to appease the furnace. He folds flour, water, baking soda and eggs into one big biscuit, and accidentally adds yeast. Room's flaming sphere rises under the table, proofs the dough, burns the table to ash and carries the biscuit into the oven. Then everyone works on the floor drains. Lumi's giant gyoza and the Hellfire Warhammer break one grate, Basil's rope monster of the deep crits through another, and eldritch blast takes a third. Room summons a fey spirit, which misses, sulks, and then breaks the last grate. The water drains away and the fey notes that now, at least, it's a dry heat.

The steam keeps coming, and a badly burned Lumi drinks his potion of supreme healing. The oven is covered in decoy controls. Lumi finds the one real lever, wipes away the soot, and reads it: "safeguards", set to off. He switches the safeguards on. The oven shuts down, its door swings open, and inside sits one giant, golden biscuit, four times the size of a normal one. Lumi shares it out. Room's cure wounds cools Basil's smoldering burns, and a potion of greater healing turns up on a shelf just when Basil needs it. Maybe Room is getting a grip on his dream.

Through a circular doorway, a vault door waits, covered in dials, levers, runes and gems. Basil's ghostly gaze sees through it to a young dragon, about the size of a horse. *"This is no big deal, right?"* Basil assures the fey spirit it serves a powerful archdruid, and the fey believes him. Their fumbling with the levers wakes the dragon, until Basil finds the lever marked "open or shut." The door swings open on a blast of cold air. Inside, black frost coats the crates, and a tunnel burrows in from the back, missing from Mario's model. The mound in the middle uncurls into leathery wings: *"Outsiders, you dare to invade my nest?"* Is the recipe in there? Where is Periwinkle? And can three battered dreamers take a dragon? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
