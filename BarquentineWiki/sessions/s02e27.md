# What's the Worst That Could Happen

*S2E27 · recorded date unknown · source: `Barquentine! S2E27 Whats the worst that could happen.transcript.vtt`*

Lumi heads back to deal with what's in his room, and the muffled wrestling starts. Basil and Room go looking for Mr. Catastrophe's friend at the Gilded Comet Conclave, a club for magic users. The gnome doorman relents only after Basil's invisible hand yanks his beanie over his face. Inside, the receptionist asks for a name. Basil gives his, and all that comes out is empty wind.

He tries again, then his full string of names. The receptionist books them as first mate Room and associated captain. Then Room remembers the Hush, where Basil told the Ledger he had given his first name. A nickname still works, and so does a remark about his favorite herb, but never his name. When Basil tries to say it, Room sees protective magic pulse around him like a heartbeat.

The host leads them to an elf with olive skin and long silver hair. He has a gift: a mangonel for the ship. Call him Mr. Catastrophe. Room's gut says he isn't lying. A Giff without a name, he says, is a Giff in form only, with no house. *"the word of a man is as good as the name that carries."* His read on the Hush is blunt: the Ledger took Basil at his word. Six hundred years, and he doesn't know what the Ledger weaves.

Back aboard, the coin in Basil's purse, a token of a debt still owed, begins to ring like a wet wine glass. King Bhurgher's voice comes through it. He once saved them from a kraken's gullet, and now his personal chef has been crushed in a meatball avalanche. The crew will cook in his name at Supper Nova. A map of portals and a golden ticket spit out of the coin. The contest is at the Flotilla, in the Astral Plane. Basil's parents, legs for the ship, the King's debt: it's all in one place. Basil was braced for one of the hells. *"what's the worst that could happen at a food competition?"* Can a Giff with no name stand before the Flotilla? Who is Captain Zyra? And what is the worst that could happen? Find out this time, on Barquentine!
