## Summary

The crew split paths at the [[Spelljammers' Supernova]]. [[Room]] and [[Lumi]] entered the arena as the **Supper Gyozas**, representing [[King Buhrghur]] in a cooking competition before thousands of screaming spectators. Meanwhile, [[Basil|the Captain]], still weakened from his infection, investigated his parents' tribunal—charges ostensibly about using a weather-controlling artifact in defiance of [[Giff]] tradition, though rumors suggest the charges are a cover story for something far more dangerous. In the arena, Room and Lumi gathered magical ingredients and prepared a cheesy blink crab ravioli with a spicy-sweet ragu. Lumi invoked his amulet, summoning a divine aspect—his aunt, the goddess of agriculture and harvest—who blessed the dish. Across the station, [[Basil|the Captain]] confronted the waiting crowd outside the [[War Saint]]'s ship, removed his mask, and declared his survival, earning an audience with the [[War Saint]] himself. Both threads end on cliffhangers.

## Key Events
- [[Room]] and [[Lumi]] compete in the arena as the **Supper Gyozas** on behalf of [[King Buhrghur]]
- The team selects four of five magical ingredients: [[comet peppers]], [[mimic dough]], [[lumen fruit]], and [[blink crab]]; rival teams pursue the blink crab but Lumi secures the mimic dough through persuasion
- Room and Lumi prepare a cheesy blink crab ravioli with a spicy-sweet ragu
- Lumi whispers sacred words to his [[amulet]], summoning a divine aspect—his aunt, the [[goddess of agriculture and harvest]]—in the holy steam above the pot
- The goddess pinches Lumi's cheek and sprinkles joy into the pot, blessing the dish
- [[Basil|the Captain]] investigates his parents' tribunal; charges are nominally about using a [[weather-controlling artifact]] in defiance of [[Giff]] tradition
- Rumors suggest the tribunal was called for the entire [[flotilla]] and the charges are a cover story; speculation that his parents stumbled onto something larger and someone wanted them silenced
- [[Basil|the Captain]] learns the [[War Saint]] is preparing testimony about a secretly developed weapon
- [[Basil|the Captain]] travels to the [[War Saint]]'s ship, removes his mask before the assembled crowd, and announces: *"The War Saint deemed me a lost cause. And yet here I am, alive and well."*
- The [[War Saint]] opens his door personally and says: *"Welcome back. You may enter."*
