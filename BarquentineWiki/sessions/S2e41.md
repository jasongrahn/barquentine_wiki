## Summary

[[The Manticore]] limped into the [[Giff]] flotilla carrying two dying crew and [[Basil|the Captain]] bearing no name at the gangplank. The [[Cartomancer]]'s cure lifted the infection but laid a curse upon [[Basil|the Captain]]'s future — a moment that should go spectacularly will instead go terribly. Cured but diminished, [[Basil|the Captain]] faced the tribunal of his parents, [[Alistair Melville]] and [[Victoria Melville]], visiting them masked in the brig as an investigator of the [[War Saint]]. They did not recognize their own son. [[Alistair Melville|Alistair]] confessed to developing a weapon in secret, insisting the artifact — a sphere found on a scarred planet, controlled through will alone — was not the weapon itself but merely a key. Diplomacy failed entirely. [[Basil|the Captain]]'s childhood friend read his own intelligence report back at him like a verdict before departing to prepare the banishment. [[Room]] had been imprisoned alongside the parents after a wild-shape escape went awry.

Six guards marched [[Basil|the Captain]] and [[Lumi]] into the brig. [[Basil|the Captain]] opened with a blast of cold; [[Lumi]] summoned Spirit Guardians, necrotic light pouring from his amulet, and banished what he could. [[Room]] broke out of his cell and tackled the last fleeing guard before he could raise the alarm. Five of the six guards died. [[Basil|the Captain]] unlocked his parents' cell — [[Alistair Melville|Alistair]] recognized his face but could not place why, for there was no name left to connect it to.

The jailbreak map unfolded: ship to ship through the flotilla's guts, two ticking clocks counting failed encounters and total steps. They pushed through a locked cargo hatch, navigated past search parties, and confronted a [[Mercane]] who threatened to telepathically broadcast their position to every merchant on the flotilla. [[Basil|the Captain]] drew upon [[The Manticore]] itself, and when he spoke, two voices came out layered — his own and the ship's. The [[Mercane]] resisted; [[Lumi]] banished it anyway.

At an unmanned medic station, [[Alistair Melville|Alistair]] and [[Victoria Melville|Victoria]] stopped running. They held hands and hummed a song from [[Basil|the Captain]]'s childhood — bassy, incomplete with only two voices, like wind through a field of grain. Then they stopped, and directed the crew: the sphere is in an iron chest in the [[Captain's Lounge]].
